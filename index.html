Why the locator matters

Money never moves directly

NSFDC does not lend to citizens. It lends to channel partners, who on-lend. Picking the wrong partner is the single most common reason an application dies.

NSFDC
Section 8 company under MoSJE
lends at 1.5–4%
Channel Partners
SCAs · PSBs · RRBs · NBFC-MFIs
on-lend at 4–15%
Beneficiary
SC entrepreneurs & students
repays the partner
From the problem statement: “Direct loan applications are not entertained. Instead, funds are routed through a Channel Finance System comprising over 100 Channel Partners” — and applications must not be sent to partners with high NPAs or overdues.
